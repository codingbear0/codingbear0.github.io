> [!quote] This productive "pile-making" is the most humorous<though simultaneously serious<aspect of the Fieldstone Method, which Weinberg refers to as the FLUB rule<the " For Later Use Bin." What you can't use in this book or article, put in a "bin" or folder on your desktop to use elsewhere, perhaps even to generate another, never-before-considered, project. 

<BR />

For Later Use Bin, FLUB:
- ‘재활용 상자’.
- Gerald Weinberg의 책 [[/books/weinberg-on-writing|Weinberg on Writing]]에서 언급.
- 현재 글에서 사용하지 못하는 아이디어나 문장을 저장해 두었다가 다른 곳에 활용하기 위한 것.
